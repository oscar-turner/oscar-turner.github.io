Research Experience
======

**Galaxy Quenching in the MaNGA Survey**  
*Haverford College · May 2026–Present*  
Advised by Prof. Karen L. Masters and Dr. Kelly Sanderson

I work on low-ionization emission regions, tracers of the end of star formation in galaxies, and use resolved maps of these regions from the MaNGA data to analyze patterns and dynamics of galaxy quenching in a sample of 10,000+ galaxies. Developed a Python code to classify the full MaNGA sample based on ionization morphology and performed data analysis on this classified sample. We find evidence for complex processes of quenching dependent on stellar mass and environment, with different mechanisms active at different galactic radii. Paper in prep.

**Analysis of Supernova Candidate AT2024nzx**  
*Yale University, Yale Summer Program in Astrophysics · June–August 2024*  
Advised by Dr. Michael Faison and Mr. Michael Warrener

One of 32 high school juniors selected out of 1000+ international applicants for a 6-week research intensive at Yale. Performed nightly observation of an unusual supernova candidate located near the galactic plane, which typically obscures supernovae. Worked in a team of 4 students to analyze the transient, performing data analysis and eventually reclassifying it as a cataclysmic variable. Learned Python, AstroImageJ, Siril, and telescope operation techniques equivalent to a semester course in observational astronomy and data science.
